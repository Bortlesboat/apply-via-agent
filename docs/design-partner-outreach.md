# Design Partner Outreach

## Target Account Criteria

Prioritize companies that meet most of these conditions:

- 200-2,000 employees
- hiring for product, engineering, design, data, or GTM knowledge-work roles
- already using Greenhouse or Ashby
- strong inbound applicant volume
- likely to attract AI-assisted or agent-assisted applicants
- modern enough to try a narrow workflow pilot without a full enterprise procurement cycle

## Priority Segments

Start with segments where applicant volume and AI-forward behavior are both likely:

- AI tooling and developer infrastructure companies
- product-led B2B SaaS companies
- high-growth startups with visible hiring brands
- recruiting-tech-adjacent companies that understand the pain early

Avoid giant enterprise-first targets at the beginning. The goal is fast feedback, not maximum logo size.

## Buyer and Champion Titles

Buyer titles:

- Head of Talent
- VP of Talent
- Recruiting Operations Lead
- Talent Systems Lead

Champion titles:

- Senior Recruiter
- Recruiting Manager
- Talent Programs Lead
- Recruiting Coordinator with ops influence

For smaller companies, the hiring founder or COO can be the buyer and champion at the same time.

## Outreach Sequence

Use a short, founder-led sequence:

1. warm intro if available
2. short cold email with one provocative line and a clear ask
3. LinkedIn note if the email gets no response
4. one follow-up message with the repo and example flow
5. stop after that unless interest appears

The ask is not "buy our recruiting platform." It is "help us shape the official agent intake channel for hiring."

## Sample Cold Email

Subject: official channel for AI applicants

Hi {{FirstName}},

Candidate AI is already applying to jobs, but right now it mostly sneaks through human web forms and creates low-trust inbound data for talent teams.

We're building Apply via Agent: an official machine channel for job applications that sits in front of your ATS, accepts structured agent-submitted applications, and adds consent, provenance, and duplicate checks before anything hits recruiter workflow.

We're looking for a small number of design partners already feeling this pain. If you're open, I'd love to show you the repo and walk through what a pilot on a few live roles could look like.

Open to a 20-minute conversation next week?

Best,
Andre

## Sample LinkedIn Note

Building something for talent teams dealing with AI-assisted applicants. The idea is simple: instead of forcing agents through human forms, give them an official, structured intake path into the ATS. If that pain is showing up on your side, I'd love to compare notes.
