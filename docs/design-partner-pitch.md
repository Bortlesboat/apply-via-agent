# Design Partner Pitch

## Who This Is For

This is for talent teams that are already seeing AI-assisted applicants and want a better option than hidden automation through human forms.

## What We Install

We add an `Apply via Agent` intake path in front of your existing ATS, define the job schema for selected roles, accept structured application packets, and attach trust metadata before syncing accepted applications into your workflow.

## Why It Matters

The current hiring channel turns AI use into noisy, low-trust inbound data. A declared machine channel gives recruiters cleaner payloads, better provenance, and a more auditable intake flow.

## Pilot Ask

Give us a small set of live roles, let us integrate with your current ATS workflow, and help us shape the trust and recruiter-review layer. In return, you get early leverage on a problem that is already showing up in your funnel.

## Pilot Success Metrics

The first pilot should be judged by a few clear questions:

- did recruiters trust these submissions more than hidden AI-assisted form submissions?
- did the gateway catch duplicates or low-quality resubmissions before they hit the ATS?
- did structured payloads make follow-up and review faster?
- would the team keep `Apply via Agent` turned on for at least some roles?

We do not need enterprise-scale rollout for the first pilot. We need signal that the intake channel is directionally better.

## What We Need From You

The ideal design partner gives us:

- a small set of active roles
- access to the current recruiter workflow and ATS shape
- one owner from Recruiting Ops or Talent Systems
- honest feedback on what trust metadata and follow-up behavior actually matter

The goal is not to create extra work for the hiring team. The goal is to learn what a production-worthy official agent channel needs to earn trust quickly.
