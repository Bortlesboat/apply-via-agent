# Design Partner Pitch

## Who This Is For

This is for talent teams that are already seeing AI-assisted applicants and want a better option than hidden automation through human forms. The best early partners are teams hiring knowledge workers, already running on Greenhouse or Ashby, and feeling the pain of high-volume, low-trust inbound applications.

## What We Install

We add a managed `Apply via Agent` intake path in front of your existing ATS. For a small set of roles, we:

- define the machine-readable job schema
- accept structured agent-submitted application packets
- translate reusable applicant data into your hiring schema
- attach trust metadata before syncing accepted applications into your workflow
- keep the existing human form available during the pilot

## Why It Matters

The current hiring channel turns AI use into noisy, low-trust inbound data. A managed machine channel gives recruiters cleaner payloads, better provenance, less duplicate noise, and a more auditable intake flow without asking the team to replace its ATS.

## Pilot Ask

Give us a small set of live roles, let us work alongside your current ATS workflow, and help us shape the trust, translation, and recruiter-review layer. In return, you get early leverage on a problem that is already showing up in your funnel.

## Pilot Success Metrics

The first pilot should be judged by a few clear questions:

- did recruiters trust these submissions more than hidden AI-assisted form submissions?
- did the translation layer map reusable applicant data cleanly into your existing workflow?
- did the gateway catch duplicates or low-quality resubmissions before they hit the ATS?
- did structured payloads make follow-up and review faster?
- would the team keep `Apply via Agent` turned on for at least some roles?

We do not need enterprise-scale rollout for the first pilot. We need signal that the intake channel is directionally better.

## What We Need From You

The ideal design partner gives us:

- a small set of active roles
- access to the current recruiter workflow and ATS shape
- one owner from Recruiting Ops or Talent Systems
- honest feedback on what trust metadata, schema translation, and follow-up behavior actually matter

The goal is not to create extra work for the hiring team. The goal is to learn what a production-worthy official intake service needs to earn trust quickly.

## Pilot Shape

The first pilot should stay narrow:

- 3-10 open roles
- one ATS
- one internal owner
- one clear review cadence

This should feel like a contained workflow experiment, not a platform migration.

## What We Handle

On our side, we own the messy part:

- job schema setup
- intake endpoint behavior
- translation into employer fields
- duplicate detection
- consent and provenance handling
- recruiter feedback loop

Your team should not have to invent the protocol with us from scratch.
