# YC Memo

## Company

Apply via Agent is building the official machine channel for job applications. We help employers accept declared, structured, agent-submitted applications instead of forcing candidate agents through human web forms.

## Problem

Candidate-side AI job applications already exist, but they currently impersonate humans inside brittle application flows. Employers get noisy payloads, duplicates, weak provenance, and rapidly declining trust in inbound applicants.

## Solution

We provide an employer-side gateway that exposes `Apply via Agent` on jobs, receives structured application packets, verifies consent and provenance, runs dedupe and trust checks, and syncs accepted applications into the employer's ATS.

## Why Now

The category is opening because all three layers are moving at once:

- candidate automation is already real
- recruiter-side AI is entering mainstream workflows
- employers are explicitly reacting to fraud, spam, and low-trust inbound data

## Why We Win

We are not building another auto-apply bot. We are building the trusted intake layer between candidate agents and employer systems. That makes us the neutral bridge between a fast-growing behavior on the candidate side and a rising pain point on the employer side.
