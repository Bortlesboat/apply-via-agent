# Why Now

## Candidate Automation

Candidates are already using AI to search jobs, tailor materials, and mass-apply. Whether employers endorse it or not, the behavior is here.

## Employer-Side AI

Recruiter-side AI is now mainstream enough that talent leaders understand the category. That lowers the education burden for a product that sits between candidate agents and ATS workflows.

## Trust Breakdown

The real pain is not "AI exists." The pain is that the current channel hides AI behind human forms. That creates unclear provenance, duplicate applications, and lower recruiter trust in inbound candidates overall.

## Timing

This is the moment when a neutral protocol plus hosted trust layer can take shape. The market has enough urgency to care, but no clear hiring-specific standard yet owns the interaction between candidate agents and employer intake systems.
